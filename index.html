Farkle: Play with Friends

The classic 10,000 dice game for iPhone and iPad, with spoken play-by-play and turn-by-turn online games.

Get help or report a problem

Open a ticket on the support page on GitHub. Tell us your device, iOS version and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

How do I play online? Tap PLAY ONLINE on the start screen. You need to be signed in to Game Center (Settings → Game Center). Invite friends or tap “Play Someone New”.

I'm not getting turn notifications. Check Settings → Notifications → Farkle and make sure notifications are allowed.

The voice sounds robotic. Download a Premium or Enhanced English voice in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English. The app uses it automatically.

Can I change the scoring rules? Yes. Pick a preset on the start screen or tap “Customize Rules & Options”.
