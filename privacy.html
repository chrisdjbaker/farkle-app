Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy covers the Farkle: Play with Friends dice game app for iPhone, iPad and Mac (“the app”).

We don’t collect your data

The developer does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The app has no developer accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer runs no servers.

Online play (Game Center)

If you choose to play online, games are run by Apple’s Game Center using your Game Center account. The username you pick in the app (for example “LuckyDice42”), your scores and the state of each game are sent through Game Center to the other players in that game. Your real name and Apple Account are not shown to other players by the app. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. Turn notifications are delivered by Apple; you can turn them off in Settings → Notifications.

What stays on your device

The app saves your player names, chosen rules and sound settings on your device only, so they are there the next time you play. This information never leaves your device, and deleting the app removes it.

Speech

Spoken announcements are generated on your device by Apple’s built-in text-to-speech. The app does not record or listen to audio.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
